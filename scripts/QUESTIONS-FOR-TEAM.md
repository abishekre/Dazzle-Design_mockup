## Part 3 · How to get the technical bits

### WhatsApp alerts — the CallMeBot key (2 minutes, per person)

Each person who wants enquiries pinged to their phone does this **once**:

1. Save **+34 644 59 71 67** as a contact (call it anything, e.g. "Website Alerts")
2. From **their own** phone, send that contact a WhatsApp message with exactly:
   `I allow callmebot to send me messages`
3. It replies with a line containing **`apikey=1234567`**
4. Send me that number, plus which phone it belongs to

Send me the pair like: `2819228650 → 1234567`

> **Why this way:** the official WhatsApp Business API needs Meta business
> verification and pre-approved message templates — heavy setup for what is just
> "ping our own phones". CallMeBot is free and does exactly this. The trade-off:
> each person must opt in once, as above.

### Instagram URL

On the My Floral profile → **⋯ menu → Copy profile URL**, or just send me the
`@handle` and I'll build the link.

### Photos

Any of these work, easiest first:

1. **Send them on WhatsApp / Google Drive** — I'll crop and optimise
2. **Instagram export** — if the account has lots of posts, run the script at
   `scripts/fetch-instagram.py` (see `scripts/FETCH-README.md`) and share the folder
3. **Straight from the phone gallery** — originals are best; screenshots lose quality

Useful to know per photo: what event it was, and whether the customer is OK with
it being shown publicly (especially if children are in it — communions,
baptisms, first birthdays).

### Testimonials

Simplest ask to a past customer:

> on our new website — first name only, or anonymous if you prefer."

A forwarded WhatsApp screenshot is fine — I'll type it up.
